Good questions

Everything you might ask.

Is it really free? ＋

Yes — free forever, no card required. We built Table to be genuinely useful, not to upsell you.

Do I need to cook every night? ＋

Not at all. Turn on "cook once, eat twice" and Table plans leftovers for you, doubling the right dinners so nothing goes to waste.

Can I swap meals I don't like? ＋

Tap ✕ on any dish to swap it instantly, or browse categories and add your own favorites to any day.

Where do the recipes link to? ＋

Every dish links to a real recipe search — no dead links, no fabricated pages. What you see is what you can cook.

Does my data stay mine? ＋

Always. Your profile and plans are yours; we never sell your data. You can export or delete everything anytime.
