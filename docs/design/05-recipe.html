Miso salmon grain bowl

Sticky miso-glazed salmon over nutty grains, quick-pickled cucumber and a sesame drizzle. Weeknight-fast, dinner-party good.

▶ Cook
⏱ 25 min 👐 Easy 620 kcal 38 g protein Cook double

Ingredients

2

servings · scales the list

  • Salmon fillets2 × 140 g
  • Brown rice or farro160 g
  • White miso paste2 tbsp
  • Honey1 tbsp
  • Cucumber½
  • Rice vinegar1 tbsp
  • Edamame100 g
  • Sesame seeds1 tsp
  • Spring onion2
See full recipe ↗

Method

  1. Cook the grains per pack. Whisk miso, honey and a splash of water into a glaze.
  2. Thinly slice cucumber; toss with rice vinegar and a pinch of salt to quick-pickle.
  3. Brush salmon with the glaze. Grill or pan-sear skin-side down 4 min, flip 2 min, basting.
  4. Warm the edamame. Build bowls: grains, salmon, edamame, pickles.
  5. Drizzle remaining glaze, scatter sesame and spring onion. Bon appétit.
